
# Alpin – Sustainability and Engineering

![Alpin ESG Advisory Infographic](./Alpin-%20ESG%20Advisory.png)

Alpin is a global leader in sustainability and engineering, specialising in ESG reporting, Acoustic Engineering, carbon emissions calculations, and environmental product declarations. As an Independent Commissioning Authority, Alpin ensures project quality and sustainability while supporting clients with [sustainability reporting](https://www.alpinme.com/solutions/sustainability-reporting/) and decarbonization strategies.

Over the past decade, Alpin has evolved into a comprehensive provider, delivering commercially viable, sustainable projects through a trusted network of world-class consultants and regional project managers. Headquartered in Masdar City, Abu Dhabi, with offices in Dubai, Kuwait, and Saudi Arabia, Alpin also collaborates with global consultants in Paris and London.

Since joining Setec in 2020, Alpin has taken on complex global projects, including LEED certification, supporting clients in achieving [green building certification](https://www.alpinme.com/solutions/green-building-certification/) and advancing a more sustainable, secure, and connected world through innovation and environmental responsibility.
